# Resolution Trend & Escalation Intelligence

Phase 1.14 tracks recorded resolution history over time.

## Measures
- Daily recorded resolution events.
- Day-over-day changes when two observed days exist.
- Recorded resolved, blocked, reopened and validation-pending events.
- Reopened and repeated-history signals for human review.

## Evidence boundary
Missing days are not zeros. Trend changes are descriptive and do not predict outcomes or establish causality, severity, root cause, or business impact. Escalation signals never trigger automatic remediation.

## Schedule
Daily at 12:15 UTC (15:15 Kuwait), plus manual execution.

## Safety
No retries, code changes, merges, publishing, spending, trading, or external messages.
