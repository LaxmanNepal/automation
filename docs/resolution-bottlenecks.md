# Resolution Trend & Bottleneck Intelligence

Phase 1.13 provides descriptive signals from the Resolution Ledger.

## Measures
- Current resolution-state counts.
- Unresolved age from the first recorded history event.
- Average and oldest unresolved age.
- Concentration of unresolved records by workflow and stage.
- Repeated history-event concentration.

## Interpretation boundary
A concentration or aging signal is not a causal bottleneck diagnosis. It does not establish root cause, remediation quality, productivity, or business impact. Missing timestamps remain unavailable, not zero.

## Schedule
Runs daily at 12:00 UTC (15:00 Kuwait) and manually on demand.

## Safety
No retries, code changes, merges, publishing, spending, trading, or external messages are performed.
